Privacy Policy

What StoryStats collects

When you download a stats card, we log the reading-session details you entered: pages or time spent, percent complete, the optional book title, the optional custom stat you chose, your ink color and background choice, mode (physical/audiobook/ebook), and orientation, along with basic technical info (device type, OS, browser, timestamp, and how you reached the page). This goes into a private Silent Book Club spreadsheet used to run the tool and to understand aggregate reading trends (e.g., how many people are reading vs. listening). It is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone outside Silent Book Club.

Book covers you upload

If you upload a photo of a book cover, it's processed entirely inside your own browser to draw your card. It is never uploaded, transmitted, or stored anywhere. We don't have a copy of it and never see it.

Email signup

The optional email signup is used only to send you the Silent Book Club newsletter, and only if you opt in. You can unsubscribe at any time. This is governed by SBC's main Privacy Policy.

Cookies & local storage

StoryStats stores a couple of small technical values in your browser (not third-party ad-tracking cookies): one to avoid double-counting if you download the same card twice, and one to remember that you've already seen the email signup prompt. We also use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand overall usage of the tool, including pages viewed, buttons clicked, general device/browser type, and an approximate location derived from IP address, per Google's standard practice. See Google's own privacy documentation for how they handle that data.

AI disclosure

StoryStats was built with the help of AI coding tools (credited in the footer below). We don't use AI to process, profile, or make decisions about your personal information. The tool just draws a card from the numbers you type in.

What we don't collect

No accounts, no passwords, no payment information, no precise location, no health data. We don't knowingly collect information from children, and StoryStats isn't directed at children.

How long we keep it & your rights

Session data is kept to power the tool and its aggregate reading-trend reporting. Because StoryStats doesn't use accounts or logins, we can't look up or delete "your" data by username, but if you've shared identifying details (like an email or a specific book title/timestamp you remember) and want it removed, email hello@silentbook.club and we'll act on it. You can also ask what we hold, correct it, or object to how it's used at the same contact.
